# Repository governance

## Roles
afflom (repository admin) is the accountable owner; afflom and Knapp-Kevin are maintainers with decision authority for their areas, and afflom decides when they disagree.

## Decisions
Every change reaches main through a pull request that passes just vv in the pinned SDK image and is approved by the accountable owner.

## Amendments
Only the accountable owner may amend governance policy or authorize an exception, recorded in a pull request that states the change or exception and its scope.
